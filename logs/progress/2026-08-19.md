## Problems

### Excel is the entire remaining gap

- `single_pdf` **79–85** in every run; `single_excel` **34–48**, `multi_excel` **35–43**, `combined` **27–59**.
- **16 of 32 items need a value computed out of the workbook.**
- `correctness` ~1.5/4 and `completeness` ~1.4/4 — 45% of rubric weight, unmoved by every intervention. Meanwhile `clarity` 3.5 and `groundedness` 2.8: it writes well and stays in its evidence, it just gets the numbers wrong.

### Root causes, in order

1. **`excel_card` chunks are catalogue descriptors with no values** ("Stored records: 128"). The model was shown a *description* of the data and asked for the data — so it correctly refused. Partially fixed by row evidence.
2. **Routing returned `need_excel=False` on all 32 questions.** `_EXCEL_RE` demands a literal `workbook`/`QDR`/`Q[1-4]`, which no stakeholder phrasing uses.
3. **9 of 16 Excel questions died on `MIN_CARD_SCORE = 0.30`** while the *correct* card scored 0.03–0.10. A cross-encoder trained on passage relevance cannot score terse catalogue text; the threshold was uncalibratable. Removed.
4. **The planner could emit only one query**; several questions need two (a total plus the ranked detail). Now fixed — works mechanically, neutral on score.
5. **What is left is semantic, not structural**: picking the wrong metric from a family (`duration_of_X` vs `frequency_of_X`), cross-table joins, and inventing figures for periods the workbook does not cover.

### Not the cause

- **Retrieval / rerank.** The bigger Qwen reranker scored *worse* (47.46 vs 50.16) at 6× the latency. The only rerank-adjacent fix that mattered was *deleting* a threshold, not ranking better. Errors are "right document, wrong number", not top-k misses.
- **The embedding swap was never isolated** — it shipped bundled with the coverage fix in the re-ingest, and most of that +7.8 is probably the coverage fix, not the encoder.

### Open issues

- **Abstention vs fabrication is the score's hinge.** Pushing the model to answer buys Excel points and pays them back ~1:1 as fabrication on unanswerable items. Neither prompt direction wins; stating absence as fact didn't help either.
- **Run-to-run noise is ±3 points** (same config scored 51.37 and 54.53). Single-run A/B cannot work — every future claim needs ≥3 replicates per arm.
- **The two graders differ by 17 pts on identical answers.** "Above 80" is not a testable target until it is settled which scale it refers to. On the human scale, current status is roughly high-60s.
