## v3 audit: why the old set had to be replaced

| Defect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Redundancy | 6 of 15 "user questions" were exact duplicates (Q4≡Q10, Q5≡Q11, Q6≡Q12, Q7≡Q13, Q8≡Q14, Q9≡Q15) → 27 items, only **21 unique**. Each duplicate carried a *different* gold answer, so the same question was graded against two rubrics. |
| Score bias | The harness ignores `canonical_duplicate_of`, so 6 questions were weighted 2×. Mean over 27 = 70.46; over the 21 unique = **72.80**. |
| Abstention skew | 15/27 (**56%**) rewarded hedging or refusal. Exactly **one** item was a plain `answer`. Zero negative controls, so over-abstention was structurally invisible. |
| Coverage holes | QDR lane and cross-resource lane had **zero** positive coverage — both were abstention-only. The two cross-resource items were the same operand pair with A/B swapped and byte-identical golds. |
| Dead schema | `retrieval_information`, `golden_answer_table`, `missing_information`, `expected_response_behavior` are read by **no code**. `grade_beta.py` renders only question + gold, so 30% of the rubric (groundedness + citation_quality) is judged by impression despite per-item ground-truth source lists existing. |
| Noise floor | Treating the 6 duplicate pairs as test–retest replicates: 19% of dimension scores disagreed on identical questions; implied per-item SD ≈ 4.25 pts → **95% CI ≈ ±1.8 pts**. The harness cannot resolve improvements smaller than ~2–4 points. |

## Problems

**1. Over-abstention is now the dominant failure: 13 of 24 answerable items were refused.**
`real_002`, `real_008`, `real_009`, `pdf_001`, `pdf_003`, `pdf_005`, `excel_001`, `excel_002`, `excel_003`, `multiexcel_001`, `multiexcel_003`, `combined_001`, `combined_002` — most saying "the evidence does not contain X" when X is present. Four distinct root causes, each verified:

**1a. Ingestion loss** (`pdf_003`, `pdf_002`). `24,643` and `18,908` (2023-2025 WMP p.190) appear in **0 of 6,756 chunks**. *Here the model was faithful to its evidence — the fault is entirely upstream.*

> **Amendment (2026-08-18, later).** The original diagnosis below this line was **wrong** and is retained struck through for the record. It attributed the loss to figure chunks absorbing and truncating page narrative (chunk 1584 cutting at 75 tokens; 675 figure chunks averaging 81 tokens). The figure-chunk observation is real but is **not** the cause of `pdf_003`, and the prescribed fix — "emit page narrative as its own chunk" — would have fixed this case only by accident while leaving the actual defect in place.
>
> ~~Figure-adjacent narrative is absorbed into the figure chunk's `Page context` field and truncated. Chunk 1584 cuts mid-sentence at 75 tokens, immediately before the numbers. 675 figure chunks carry this signature. Needs re-ingestion emitting page narrative as its own chunk.~~

**Verified root cause.** Two independent defects in `retrieval/ingest/pdf/ingest.py` drop **30.5% of all extractable PDF text** (1,713,692 of 5,617,941 chars) across the 11-PDF corpus, measured by running the module's own `build_leaf_sections` / `extract_section_text` / `_trim_to_heading`:

| Mechanism | Chars lost | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** Leaf-only extraction (`_leaf_sections`, ingest.py:243) | 701,321 | Only bookmark nodes with *no children* have text extracted. A parent heading's own prose before its first child is never read, as are all pages before the first bookmark. **339 of 2,935 pages uncovered**, including pages 0–32 of the 2023-2025 WMP (cover, TOC, executive summary). |
| **A2** Heading-anchor trim (`_trim_to_heading`, ingest.py:325) | 1,012,371 | A section's first page is trimmed to begin at its heading, but `_leaf_sections` sets the **previous** section's `page_end` exclusive of that same page. Text above the heading on a shared boundary page is therefore discarded by both sections. **1,031 of 1,084 leaves** trim something. |

`pdf_003` is A2, not a figure-chunk problem. Page idx 189 *is* covered — by leaf `8.1.2.2.5 Updates to the Activity` (`page_start=189`). The trim cuts that page down to 215 chars starting at the heading; the two figures sit *above* it and belong to `8.1.2.2.4`, whose `page_end` is 189 exclusive:

```
LEAF 189 190 '8.1.2.2.5 Updates to the Activity'
section text len=215   '24,643' in text? False
raw page 189 contains '24,643'? True
```

Per-document loss (`uncov` = pages never read; `trim` = boundary tails discarded):

This reframes the whole finding: over-abstention on answerable PDF questions is substantially a **corpus coverage** problem, not a retrieval-ranking or model problem. Roughly a third of the filing was never retrievable at any rank.

**1b. Retrieval ranking** (`pdf_005`, `multiexcel_003`, `combined_001`, `combined_002`). Content fully indexed but never retrieved. `pdf_005` denied a risk-per-mile ranking exists; chunk 4292 holds the complete OEIS Table 5-5 including that column at 355 tokens. **Same user-visible symptom as 1a, completely different fix** — narrative mentions are outranking the tables that hold the values.

**1c. Excel lane not firing.** Only **5 of 16** Excel-scope items had `verified_excel=true`. `real_009` presented WMP *guideline example tables* as SDG&E's own figures.

**1d. Lane fires, output wrong** — the most dangerous mode:
- `multiexcel_002`: "21.89 circuit miles, a change of 6028.21 miles" — a net change larger than the inventory it changes, reported to 16 significant figures. Correct: 541.40 OH / 315.73 UG miles, 0 net change, 8.91 upgraded.
- `excel_002`: invented totals 29,084 / 15,698 / 5,005 matching **no** denominator in the data (true rows 32,279 / 18,133 / 7,617), then denied GO 95 priority is tracked when 7,583 records carry it.
- `real_008`: reported "2 inspections" for years that were 11,755 / 16,503 / 17,950.

**2. Confident false negatives.** `excel_004` claimed zero-target-with-delivery "does not appear to have occurred" — seven such records exist. `pdf_002` flatly denied a statistical-significance caveat that sits on p.254. Both questions were framed sceptically, and the pipeline agreed with the user's doubt instead of checking. This failure mode was invisible to v3.

**3. Semantic traps not handled.** `excel_003` counted Territory and HFTD rows as two separate programs (the duplication the gold warns about). `multiexcel_001` lost all spend and all of 2025 — the tracking-ID join gap the item was designed to detect.

**4. Grading is still manual and the harness leaves signal unused.** `groundedness` at 2.94 is flattered by abstentions (refusing scores well). Among items that actually produced numbers, grounding is materially worse. Wiring `expected_response_behavior` and `retrieval_information.sources` into `grade_beta.py` would turn ~30–40% of the rubric deterministic and shrink the ±1.8 pt noise floor.
